# My Favorite Series: *Stranger Things*
### Introduction
My favorite series is **Stranger Things**, a science fiction and horror television series created by The Duffer Brothers.

### About the Series
The story takes place in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana. It follows a group of friends who encounter supernatural events and mysterious experiments.

### Main Characters
Some of my favorite characters are:

1. **Eleven** – A girl with supernatural abilities.
2. **Mike Wheeler** – One of Eleven's closest friends.
3. **Dustin Henderson** – The funny and intelligent member of the group.
4. **Lucas Sinclair** – A brave and loyal friend.
5. **Max Mayfield** – A strong and independent character.

### Why I Like This Series
I like Stranger Things because:

- It has an interesting storyline.
- The characters have strong friendships.
- It combines mystery, adventure, and science fiction.
- Every season has exciting plot twists.
  
>"Friends don't lie."
